# GDP Indicator Definition

## Name
Gross Domestic Product (GDP)

## Description
The total market value of all final goods and services produced within a country's territory during a specific period. Measured in nominal terms (current prices) or real terms (constant prices adjusted for inflation).

## Unit
Current USD (nominal), constant USD (real), % change YoY

## Categories
- economic-indicators
- macroeconomics
- national-accounts

## Sources
- Statistics Finland (national GDP data)
- Eurostat (EU comparison data)
- OECD (regional GDP estimates)
- World Bank (global GDP comparisons)

## Methodology
National accounts follow the UN System of National Accounts (SNA 2008) standards. GDP is calculated using the expenditure approach: GDP = C + I + G + (X - M), where C is consumption, I is investment, G is government spending, X is exports, and M is imports.

## Known Limitations
- Does not account for informal economy or unpaid work
- Quality varies by country's statistical capacity
- Revision lag: preliminary estimates are often revised significantly
- Exchange rate effects on nominal GDP comparisons
