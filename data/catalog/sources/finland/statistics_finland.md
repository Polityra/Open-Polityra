## Description

Statistics Finland is the central Finnish authority for official statistics. It provides
comprehensive data on:

- Population demographics and projections
- GDP, inflation, and fiscal indicators
- Employment and labor market statistics
- Trade and economic cooperation
- Regional development indicators
- Public finance and taxation

## Relevance to Open Polityra

Finnish public finance and demographic data from Statistics Finland forms a baseline for economic
analysis. The platform will use this source as one of several reference datasets, alongside
Eurostat, OECD, World Bank, and other international sources.

## Integration Notes

- Data is available in CSV, JSON, and Excel formats via the PX-Web API
- Requires attribution to Statistics Finland in all visualizations and reports
- Some advanced datasets may require registration
