# World Bank Open Data

## Publisher

The World Bank Group

## Canonical URL

https://data.worldbank.org

## License

CC BY 4.0 - See https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/static/terms-of-use

## Update Frequency

Varies by dataset (mostly monthly, quarterly, or annual)

## Description

The World Bank provides comprehensive global development data including:

- Gross National Income (GNI) per capita
- GDP growth rates
- Inflation indicators
- Trade volumes and terms of trade
- Poverty rates and inequality metrics
- Governance indicators (Worldwide Governance Indicators)
- Infrastructure development metrics

## Relevance to Open Polityra

International economic comparisons require standardized data from the World Bank. The platform will
use World Bank data as a baseline for global economic indicators, particularly for cross-country
analysis of Finland's trading partners and geopolitical allies.

## Integration Notes

- API endpoint: https://api.worldbank.org/v2/
- Data available in JSON, CSV, Excel formats
- Wide coverage of countries (recommended minimum dataset complement)
