# Eurostat

## Publisher
European Commission - Directorate-General for Communication Networks, Content and Technology

## Canonical URL
https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat

## License
CC BY 4.0 - See https://data.europa.eu/uillm/data-licensing-guidelines

## Update Frequency
Varies by dataset (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly)

## Description
Eurostat is the statistical office of the European Union. It provides extensive data on:
- EU-wide GDP, inflation, and economic indicators
- Regional development across EU member states
- Labor market statistics
- Trade within the EU
- Budget and fiscal policy
- Social indicators (poverty, education, health)

## Relevance to Open Polityra
EU-level economic and social data is critical for comparing Finland's position relative to broader European trends. Eurostat provides standardized cross-country comparisons that are essential for geopolitical and economic analysis.

## Integration Notes
- Data available via API: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/main/data/database
- Most datasets available in CDATA format for programmatic access
- Regional data uses NUTS classification system
