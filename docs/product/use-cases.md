# Initial use case

## Explore Finland's fiscal balance and debt

**User:** a researcher or interested member of the public reviewing public fiscal statistics.
**Question:** how have Finland's general-government deficit/surplus and gross debt changed, and how do transparent assumptions alter illustrative debt paths?

The user can inspect annual Eurostat B9 net lending/borrowing and GD consolidated gross-debt ratios, view the source and retrieval date, and compare a baseline, configurable stress scenario, and gradual-improvement scenario.

### Boundaries

- The historical values are source observations; scenario values are deterministic calculations.
- Scenarios are not forecasts and do not express recommended policy.
- The formula omits stock-flow adjustments and other national-accounting effects.
- Source revisions and independent economics/statistics review remain relevant limitations.

Further domains and AI-assisted explanations require separate research briefs, source/license checks, evaluation plans, and review.
