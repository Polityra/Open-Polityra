# Product requirements

## Initial prototype

The first research slice focuses on public annual Finland general-government fiscal observations and transparent, illustrative debt scenarios. See the [fiscal-balance research brief](../../research/domains/finnish-economy/fiscal-balance-brief.md).

The prototype should:

- identify source, geography, period, units, retrieval date, and indicator definitions;
- distinguish historical observations from scenario calculations;
- expose the assumptions and calculation method;
- validate user-adjustable assumptions and return visible errors for invalid input;
- avoid AI-generated factual claims and policy recommendations;
- state the data and model limitations.

## Explicit non-requirements

- It is not an official statistical or government service.
- It is not a forecast, policy recommendation, fiscal plan, or causal policy evaluation.
- It does not automate or make public decisions.
- It does not support classified or individual-level sensitive data.

The repository is an early prototype; these requirements do not imply independent review or production readiness.
