# Geopolitics Research Domain

## Scope
Analysis of international relations, power dynamics, and strategic developments affecting the security and prosperity of open societies. This domain covers:

- Great-power competition and multilateral institution effectiveness
- Regional conflicts and their geopolitical implications
- Alliance structures and defense cooperation frameworks
- Resource geopolitics (energy, critical minerals, food security)
- Normative and institutional evolution in international relations

## Data Sources
- Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (SIPRI) — military expenditure and arms transfers
- Crisis Group — conflict monitoring
- OECD Development Assistance Database — geopolitical influence indicators
- UN General Assembly voting records — diplomatic alignment analysis
- World Bank Governance Indicators

## Methodological Notes
- Geopolitical analysis must distinguish between observable events (military deployments, treaty signings) and interpreted strategic positioning.
- Sources from different geopolitical actors may present conflicting accounts of the same events; all versions should be documented where relevant.
- AI-assisted analysis in this domain requires strict human oversight to avoid confirmation bias and stereotyping.
