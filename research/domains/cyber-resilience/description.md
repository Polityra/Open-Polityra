# Cyber Resilience Research Domain

## Scope
Assessment of cybersecurity posture, digital infrastructure resilience, and the intersection between cyber threats and national security. This domain covers:

- Critical information infrastructure protection across public and private sectors
- Cyber threat landscape and attribution challenges
- Digital governance frameworks and international norms
- Emerging technology risks (AI-powered attacks, quantum computing implications)
- Public-sector digital transformation and associated vulnerabilities

## Data Sources
- ENISA (European Union Agency for Cybersecurity) — EU cyber threat landscape reports
- National Cyber Security Centre (various jurisdictions) — national threat intelligence
- NATO Cooperative Cyber Defence Centre of Excellence — incident databases
- MITRE ATT&CK framework — technique-level threat analysis
- Internet infrastructure monitoring services

## Methodological Notes
- Cyber threat data is inherently uncertain; attribution is difficult and may change over time.
- Public information provides an incomplete picture of cyber incidents due to reporting delays and classification constraints.
- Analysis should distinguish between technical indicators (malware signatures, exploit patterns) and strategic intent assessments.
