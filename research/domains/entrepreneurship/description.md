# Entrepreneurship Research Domain

## Scope
Analysis of entrepreneurship ecosystems, business formation dynamics, and innovation-driven economic growth. This domain covers:

- Startup activity and venture capital flows
- Small and medium enterprise (SME) health indicators
- Innovation capacity and patent activity
- Regional entrepreneurship clusters and networks
- Regulatory environment for business formation and growth

## Data Sources
- Statistics Finland (business registrations and exits)
- OECD Entrepreneurship Indicators
- Global Entrepreneurship Monitor (GEM)
- European Innovation Scoreboard
- World Bank Doing Business indicators (historical)

## Methodological Notes
- Entrepreneurship data quality varies significantly across jurisdictions; cross-country comparisons require caution.
- Venture capital concentration in specific regions skews aggregate analysis toward urban centers.
- New business formation data has a typical lag of 3-6 months depending on the reporting authority.
