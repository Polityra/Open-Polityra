# Finnish Economy Research Domain

## Scope
Analysis of Finland's economy, public finance, trade relationships, and economic policy. This domain covers:

- Gross Domestic Product (GDP) trends and projections
- Public finances including taxation, government expenditure, and debt sustainability
- Key sectors: technology and innovation, manufacturing, forestry, services
- Trade balances and strategic dependencies (especially EU, Nordic, and Baltic regions)
- Labor market dynamics and demographic challenges
- Regional economic disparities within Finland

## Data Sources
- Statistics Finland (Tilastokeskus) — primary national statistics source
- Bank of Finland — monetary policy, financial stability, balance of payments
- Eurostat — comparative EU data
- OECD — comparative international data
- Ministry of Finance Finland — budget forecasts and fiscal analysis

## Methodological Notes
- Finnish economy is small and open; trade exposure significantly affects macro indicators.
- Demographic trends (aging population, regional depopulation) are structurally important but difficult to model with high precision.
- Sectoral analysis should distinguish between cyclical fluctuations and structural shifts.
