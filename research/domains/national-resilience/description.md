# National Resilience Research Domain

## Scope

Analysis of national resilience across economic, social, and institutional dimensions. This domain
covers:

- Economic resilience: diversification of trade partners, supply chain robustness, fiscal buffer
  capacity
- Social resilience: community cohesion, public trust in institutions, demographic sustainability
- Institutional resilience: governance quality, regulatory adaptability, crisis management capacity
- Infrastructure resilience: energy grid redundancy, digital infrastructure backup, transport
  network robustness

## Data Sources

- OECD Resilience Framework indicators
- World Bank Governance Indicators
- National crisis management authorities (e.g., Finnish Rescue Service reports)
- European Commission Risk Assessment Reports

## Methodological Notes

- Resilience is a multidimensional construct; no single metric captures it fully.
- Composite indices should be transparent about weighting and normalization methods.
- Trend analysis should account for structural changes in governance and technology that shift
  baseline resilience.
