# Public Finance Research Domain

## Scope
Analysis of public finance sustainability, fiscal policy effectiveness, and government revenue-expenditure dynamics. This domain covers:

- Federal and sub-national government budgets
- Taxation systems and revenue sustainability
- Government debt trajectories and risk assessment
- Intergenerational equity in public finance
- Revenue from natural resources and structural transformation

## Data Sources
- Finance Ministry fiscal data (national)
- OECD Revenue Statistics
- IMF Government Finance Statistics
- Eurostat government finance data
- World Bank Government Debt Database

## Methodological Notes
- Fiscal sustainability analysis depends critically on assumptions about long-term growth rates and interest rates.
- Transitory revenues (e.g., from natural resources or one-time asset sales) should be distinguished from structural revenue.
- Ageing-related expenditure projections require careful demographic assumptions.
