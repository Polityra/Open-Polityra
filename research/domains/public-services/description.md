# Public Services Research Domain

## Scope
Analysis of public service provision, quality, accessibility, and innovation. This domain covers:

- Healthcare system performance and sustainability
- Education system outcomes and equity
- Social welfare program effectiveness
- Digital public infrastructure (e-governance, digital identity)
- Regional disparities in service access

## Data Sources
- Statistics Finland (public expenditure and outcome data)
- European Union statistics on public administration
- OECD Health and Education indicators
- World Bank Governance Indicators: Regulatory Quality, Government Effectiveness

## Methodological Notes
- Cross-country comparisons of public services require careful attention to institutional differences that affect how services are defined and measured.
- Outcome data typically lags expenditure data by 1-3 years; be explicit about lag when making comparisons.
- AI-assisted analysis of public service data must avoid reinforcing systemic inequities present in the underlying data.
