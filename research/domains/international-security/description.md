# International Security Research Domain

## Scope

Analysis of international security architecture, alliance dynamics, and strategic stability. This
domain covers:

- Nuclear deterrence and arms control regimes
- Regional security architectures and their evolution
- Defense industry trends and military modernization
- Non-traditional security threats (cyber, space, biosecurity)
- Conflict prevention and peacebuilding mechanisms

## Data Sources

- Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (SIPRI)
- International Institute for Strategic Studies (IISS)
- UN Disarmament Commission reports
- NATO strategic concepts and defense planning documents
- Regional security organization publications

## Methodological Notes

- Military capability comparisons are limited by varying definitions, classification constraints,
  and purchasing power differences.
- Arms control treaty compliance data should be sourced from official verification mechanisms where
  available.
- Strategic stability assessments require integrating multiple intelligence sources while
  acknowledging their inherent limitations.
