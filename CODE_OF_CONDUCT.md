# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OpenPolityra aims to be a respectful, evidence-led and welcoming open-source community. We welcome
participation regardless of professional background, experience level, nationality, language, age,
disability, gender, identity, religion or other personal characteristics.

We are committed to a community where people can disagree about methods, interpretations and policy
implications without harassment, intimidation or personal attacks.

## Expected behaviour

Community members are expected to:

- be respectful, considerate and constructive;
- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attack people;
- distinguish facts, interpretations, assumptions and opinions;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct errors when identified;
- give credit to authors, researchers, data publishers and contributors;
- welcome questions and explain technical or research decisions clearly;
- respect privacy, confidentiality and security boundaries;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- follow the project's data, model, security and contribution policies.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation or targeted abuse;
- doxxing, unwanted disclosure of personal information or threats to expose it;
- sustained personal attacks, insults or humiliating comments;
- deliberate disruption of meetings, reviews or community spaces;
- publishing private communications without permission;
- knowingly submitting fabricated sources, research results or contributor credentials;
- attempting to introduce malware, credentials, unauthorised access or restricted data;
- using project spaces to campaign for a political party, candidate or predetermined electoral
  outcome;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

Political and policy topics can be discussed when relevant to project research. Advocacy, harassment
and partisan campaigning are not project contributions.

## Scope

This Code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, project meetings,
official communication channels and other spaces where a person is representing or participating in
the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner or maintainers through GitHub. Do not post
sensitive details in a public issue. For security vulnerabilities, use the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as the volunteer capacity of the project permits.
The project will share information only with people who need it to assess and address the report,
subject to safety and legal obligations. Anonymous reports may be difficult to investigate, but will
be considered where sufficient information is provided.

## Possible responses

Depending on the facts and severity, maintainers may issue a reminder, request an apology or
correction, restrict participation in a discussion, temporarily suspend participation, remove
content, or exclude a person from project spaces. Serious threats or unlawful conduct may be
referred to the appropriate platform or authority.

Decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate and made without retaliation. A
person involved in a report should not be the sole decision-maker on that report.

## A note on disagreement

Good-faith disagreement, critical review and requests for evidence are encouraged. A disagreement is
not misconduct merely because it is uncomfortable or challenges an established view. Criticism
should remain relevant, specific and respectful.

## Changes

This Code may be updated through the project's documented governance process. Material changes
should be announced to contributors.
