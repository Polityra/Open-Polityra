# Privacy Notice for the Project Repository

## 1. Status and scope

This notice describes privacy expectations for participation in the Suomi Strategia AI open-source project and use of project-operated development resources.

The project is currently an early-stage initiative and does not, by this document alone, establish a hosted public service or identify a legally registered organisation as a data controller. GitHub and other infrastructure providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

If the project launches a hosted application, mailing list, event registration or other service that processes personal data, a separate service-specific privacy notice must be published before collection begins. That notice must identify the relevant controller, purposes, legal bases, data categories, recipients, retention periods, rights and contact route as applicable.

## 2. Data minimisation

Contributors should provide only information needed to participate. Do not post or commit:

- personal identity numbers, passport details or copies of identity documents;
- private addresses, personal phone numbers or private contact details without a clear need and permission;
- passwords, tokens, private keys or other credentials;
- sensitive personal data;
- private correspondence or third-party information without permission;
- personal data copied from public sources merely because it is accessible.

Public availability does not remove privacy obligations.

## 3. Public repository information

Information intentionally submitted to a public repository, issue, pull request or discussion may be visible, copied, indexed and retained by third parties. Do not publish information that should remain private. If personal information is accidentally published, contact maintainers promptly and also use the platform's relevant reporting or removal process where appropriate. Removing a file from the current branch may not remove it from Git history, forks, caches or external archives.

## 4. Application and analytics data

A future hosted application should be designed to minimise collection and retention. Before launch, maintainers must document:

- what personal data is collected and why;
- the applicable legal basis and controller responsibilities;
- whether data is shared with model or infrastructure providers;
- whether prompts, uploaded files or analysis history are retained;
- access controls, deletion processes and retention periods;
- whether data is transferred outside the European Economic Area;
- how users can exercise applicable rights.

Do not send personal, confidential, classified or sensitive public-sector information to external AI providers unless there is a documented legal, contractual, security and governance basis for doing so.

## 5. Cookies and telemetry

Any future website or application must document necessary cookies, optional analytics and telemetry before deployment. Optional tracking should not be enabled by default without an appropriate legal and consent assessment.

## 6. Security incidents and requests

For accidental exposure of personal information, contact the repository owner or maintainers privately through GitHub. For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include sensitive personal data in a public issue.

## 7. Changes

This notice must be reviewed before any hosted service or new personal-data processing activity is introduced. It is informational project guidance and is not a substitute for a service-specific legal privacy notice.
